Nintendo Switch

The Nintendo Switch is a gaming console created by Nintendo. The console itself comprises two Joy-Cons, with buttons to control the screen, which is mainly used for playing. It's a great investment for gamers, especially due to its portability and the variety of games provided. It features several free games that you can play. Some popular games, however, are not free and are usually at a higher price.
