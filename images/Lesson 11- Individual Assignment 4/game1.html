Animal Crossing

Animal Crossing is an open-world adventure game where players enjoy spending time on an island in a village. The game is relaxing and fun, allowing them to customize their island, complete objectives, and meet animal friends. Players can design and collect items, which is entirely up to them, while navigating the island. It's a cozy game that also inspires creativity!!
