The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild

In The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild, players follow the main character Link as he sets out on a journey after awakening from a century-long sleep. You help Link recover lost memories as he tries to save Hyrule. The game comprises combating your enemies, exploring a destroyed world, and helping him defeat enemies. This game is part of the Zelda game series, and would interest those who like open-world games and survival!
